About

What is this

Pulse LDN is a curated event tracker for deep house music in London. It pulls upcoming shows for a handpicked list of artists from Resident Advisor and surfaces them in one clean feed — so you can see at a glance who's playing, where, and when.

No promotions. No noise. Just the info you want.

Why it exists

Resident Advisor is great, but the events feed has way too many listings, promotions, and club nights. It's genuinely hard to track a specific artist and find the events you want. You see ads for events you want to go to on insta, but they just disappear, and by the time you remember to check, it's sold out.

Pulse LDN solves exactly that problem: one place, the artists you care about, London only.
