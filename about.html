About

What is this

Pulse LDN is a curated event tracker for deep house music in London. It pulls upcoming shows for a handpicked list of artists from Resident Advisor and surfaces them in one clean feed — so you can see at a glance who's playing, where, and when.

No promotions. No noise. Just the shows that matter.

Why it exists

Resident Advisor is great, but it wasn't built for this. The events feed is swamped with listings, promotions, and club nights — making it genuinely hard to track a specific artist across the city. Adverts for shows you actually want to go to get buried, and by the time you remember to check, it's sold out.

Pulse LDN solves exactly that problem: one place, the artists you care about, London only.

Who built it

House music and events lover. Work in AI, enjoy building things, and spend a lot of weekends chasing good sound systems around London. Always open to a conversation — about music, tech, or anything in between.
